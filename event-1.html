Fundraiser 2026

On Youth day, we remember the heroic youth that fought for the freedom we enjoy today. Looking out for each other is one of the reasons our country still stands in democracy. As a charity organization that seeks to protect childrens rights, we have organinized an event to celebrate our youth and have fun with our children while fundraising for the them to enjoy nutritious meals without any obsticles.

TT Squared Rides, a local motorcycle club, has partnered with us to host this event for our community. They will be donating food parcels to us as well. We would like everyone to participate in making this event a success on the 13th of June 2026. You can get involved in any way you deem appropriate.

To support this course, BUY A TICKET and stand a chance to WIN a kitchen accessory. We accept and appreciate any form of support, whether it's through donations, volunteering, or spreading the word about our event.

Your attendence is also a form of support. You also stand a chance of winning one of many free prices just by attending.
